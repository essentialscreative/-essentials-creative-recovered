## Email status legend

- **Verified** — email address confirmed from the org's own site or a reputable directory.
- **Likely (verify)** — standard `info@` address consistent with the domain; confirm before a formal send.
- **Use contact form** — no public inbox found; the website has a contact form / phone.
- **Check website / Facebook** — org located but no email surfaced; follow the listed URL.

## Method & caveats

- Sourced via public web searches and the organizations' own contact pages.
- Several org sites (Creative Waco, Art Center Waco, Round Rock Arts) block automated
  fetching, so their emails were confirmed from search snippets and directories rather
  than a live page read — spot-check before a formal campaign.
- "Central Texas" was scoped to the Austin–Waco–Temple corridor plus the
  Round Rock/Georgetown/San Marcos/Killeen/Salado satellites. Tell me if you want a
  different radius (e.g. add Bastrop, San Antonio, Hill Country) or only a specific
  discipline (visual arts vs. performing arts vs. arts education).
- Verify addresses against current websites before sending; org staff and inboxes change.
